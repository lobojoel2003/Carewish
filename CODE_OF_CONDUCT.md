# Code of conduct

Carewish is about one of the most personal decisions people make. Bring that same care to this project.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Carewish to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially when discussing illness, death, faith, and family.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's privacy. Never share someone's medical or personal details.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, trolling, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub (Security → Report a vulnerability works for any private report, or contact the repository owner). Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation for behavior that breaks this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
